Run on gqlforge's vendored runtime: `GPPClient`/`AsyncGPPClient` now subclass the generated client, and the exception hierarchy is the vendored one (`ClientError` base with `AuthError`, `TransportError`, `RequestTimeoutError`, `ResponseError`, `GraphQLResponseError`, `OperationUnavailableError`, `ReadOnlyError`), plus the GPP-specific `GPPConfigError`, `GPPFieldUnavailableError`, `GPPRetryableError`, and `GPPValidationError`. `GPPModel`, `GPPInput`, and `UNSET` remain importable from their usual locations.
